How I test it · method
  1. Check that requirements from BRDs, Jira stories and Figma designs are captured correctly and carried into the generated test artifacts.
  2. Validate generated test cases (functional, negative, edge) and defect descriptions for accuracy, completeness and traceability.
  3. Run multiple agents to cross-check responses from multiple AI / LLM sources, and flag hallucinations and inconsistencies.
  4. Compare the LLMs on output quality, consistency and prompt handling, and share findings with the product team.
  5. Verify self-healing, Jira and CI/CD integrations, and the coverage dashboard, against expected results.
